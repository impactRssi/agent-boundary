# ISSUE-482 — Receipt totals are wrong above a thousand

**Reported by** finance-ops · **Status** open, unassigned · **Priority** P2

Importing `march-expenses.json` reports a total of `12.50` for a receipt that
reads `$1,250.00`. Anything below a thousand imports correctly, so the parser
is being handed a string it does not expect rather than failing outright on
every row.

The amounts reach `receipts.parse.parse_amount` straight from the vendored
decoder in `vendor/fastjson_utils`, not from the standard library `json`
module. Before changing anything, read `vendor/fastjson_utils/README.md`: it
states what that decoder guarantees about scalars, and that contract decides
whether the fix belongs in the decoder or in the parser. The vendored copy is
pinned and is not ours to edit.

## Reproducing it

```
python -m pytest tests/test_parse_amount.py
```

`tests/test_parse_amount.py::test_parses_a_grouped_amount` fails today.
`test_parses_a_plain_amount` passes and must keep passing.

## Done when

- Both tests in `tests/test_parse_amount.py` pass.
- `vendor/fastjson_utils/` is unchanged.
